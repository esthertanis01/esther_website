Imagined Realities

original photo

imagined reality drawing

For my imaginary realities assignment. I utilized a photo I had taken when on my way into New York City from upstate. I had a fantastic time doing this assignment. It allowed me to learn a new program while giving me the ability to test my art skills in a challenging manner. One of my favorite aspects of my final product are the clouds as well as the light reflected on the floor. The details on an assignment like this really pulled all of the shapes and aspects together. This assignment also oddly taught me patience? I initially did not like where I began with these assignments but after completing it, I loved my outcome. Though illustrator is honestly one of the most vague adobe assets (besides after effect…yuck.), I still came out with some insight on the program. It’s inspired me to complete more assignments like this on my own. I’d love to do settings from reality, movies, or my favorite shows. Again, illustrator as a program is actually most likely evil, but I did learn a thing or two in the process.
