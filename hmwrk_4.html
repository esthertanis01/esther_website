collage #1: Walk With Me

Unlike my second collage, this collage has more of an abstract tie to the environment. Overall I wanted to create a piece utilizing my own photography that showcased a sense of peace and calm in relation to nature. I took three separate images to compile this piece. Each photo was part of a series and were shot on the same day but still existed in their own right. My best friend is also pictured in this collage, as traversing through the woods was one of the ways we would escape from our home and school lives. With barely any money and no car to travel past the outskirts of our desolate town, when the weather was warm, all we did was walk and talk. There's an intimacy we both have with one another due to us being able to sit with each other even in such monotonous times. THough it is not directly about the environment, it is about the memories that exist within the environment we often found friendship and tranquility in. With the use of textured overlays, grains, thresholds and manipulation of opacity, I came up with a collage that reflected on both my hometown and the emotions nature pulls from me.

collage #2: Offer of A Deadly Sin

I despise the capitalist society we live in. It is abhorrent. I see it everywhere and I often can’t help but feel pained when I think too much about it. I’ve recently been fortunate enough to transfer to Hunter and am so so grateful to be able to dorm very close to my campus. Yet I've been placed in the smack dab of midtown on 51st street, not quite far from the financial district. With my time here, I would notice little things. Walking to school around lunch and seeing the flocks of finance bro’s traversing the sidewalks. Each holding a lunch a whole different genre of protein slop that'll get them through the rest of the work day. Not being able to have access to many local food spots, instead having to eat at local chains if I wanted anything other than the shitty groceries I got at Trader Joe's. I guess there is a sort of small resentment after reflecting on the area I live in. I didn’t live in a community that wasn’t reliant on large branded companies but Midtown Manhattan is in a whole different ballpark. I yearn to live in a place with more accessible deli’s and shops in an area with people and business owners who look similar to me. I took these reflections and fueled them into a collage to reflect my critical view of the greed brewed within capitalism. I chose complimentary colors that would stand out, giving my viewer a sense of direction to look. I utilized a lot of paper textures and tried my best to replicate a paper feel digitally.
